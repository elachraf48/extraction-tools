IP and Email Extraction Tools

Hi there! Welcome to the IP and Email Extraction Tools website. My name is Achraf, and I'm the creator of this site. Here, you'll find powerful tools designed to make your life easier when it comes to extracting IP addresses, email addresses, and manipulating text data.

The IP Extraction Tool is a valuable resource for cybersecurity professionals, webmasters, or anyone who needs to quickly identify IP addresses. Whether you want to analyze network traffic, investigate potential security threats, or track website visitors, this tool provides a simple and efficient solution.

With the Email Extraction Tool, marketing professionals, recruiters, and researchers can save valuable time by automating the process of collecting email addresses. Whether you're building a targeted email list, conducting market research, or performing competitive analysis, this tool will help you gather email addresses from text or web pages effortlessly.

Our Text Tools section offers various utilities for text manipulation, including splitting text, removing duplicates, and sorting. The Fixage tool allows you to easily replace placeholders in your text with custom values, perfect for template-based content creation.

All tools are designed with user-friendliness in mind. You can access them easily from the navigation bar above. Simply choose the tool you need, follow the instructions on the respective page, and enjoy the benefits of streamlined data extraction and text manipulation.

Rest assured that our tools are completely free to use. We're passionate about providing accessible and efficient solutions for professionals and enthusiasts alike.

Ready to get started? Explore the power of our extraction and text tools today and take your productivity to new heights!

Privacy Policy

At Extraction Tools, we take your privacy and data security very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and tools.

Data Collection

Our tools process data that you input directly into our website. This may include text containing IP addresses, email addresses, or other content you choose to enter. We want to assure you that:

  • All data processing happens locally in your browser
  • We do not store or retain any of the text data you input
  • We do not transmit your input data to our servers
  • No personal information is collected through your use of our extraction tools

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics that help us improve our website. This information includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral source

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using browser extensions like Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These cookies may store preferences like dark mode settings or remember your usage patterns to provide a better experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for functionality like font loading (Google Fonts), icons (Font Awesome), or scripts (jQuery, Bootstrap). These services may collect some information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us through the Contact Us link in the navigation menu.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
